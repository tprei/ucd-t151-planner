Sorted by how much of your working day it eats. Click a row to drop it into the selected term.
The MSc is 90 credits: 60 taught credits plus a 30-credit project, dissertation or work placement in the summer, which is not timetabled here. The T151 curriculum tells part-time students to take about 30 taught credits a year split across Autumn and Spring. UCD Academic Regulations 3.6 and 3.7 cap a trimester at 30 required and 40 registered credits, and count one credit as roughly 25 hours of student effort (20–30). Module list and session times: T151 curriculum option lists A–E plus the “When is this module offered?” block and the Mode of Delivery field on each module page (AY 2026/27). Week numbers to calendar dates: UCD Registry week ranges and the Academic Council calendars 2025–2031. Autumn uses weeks 1–12, Spring 19–34 with the study break at 27–28. Blended modules are hidden by default and struck through when shown. Where a module title contradicts the hub’s delivery field, both are printed. Exam sittings are drawn hatched; end-of-trimester exams are left out of the work-hour counts, mid-term sittings are not.
